Variety background

A mandarin with a regional story.

The exporter reference describes Wogan (also called Orah Mandarin) as a hybrid of “Temple” orange and “Danxi” red orange. It says the variety was introduced for planting in Chongqing in 2004, and entered Guangxi in March 2012.

The same source reports that Wuming Wogan expanded rapidly in the region over the following decade. This page retains that history as supplier-provided background, rather than presenting it as an independently audited industry statistic.

Growing region

Wuming, Guangxi — the growing environment

The supplier describes Wuming as a mountainous growing area with fertile soil, organic matter and trace elements, alongside warm conditions, sunlight and rainfall suited to citrus cultivation.

What the source highlights

Soil: The exporter reports fertile soil with organic matter and naturally occurring trace elements.

Climate: The source highlights abundant light, warmth and rainfall in Wuming.

Orchard management: It describes seedling selection, planting density, crop monitoring and managed fertilisation and crop protection.

These cultivation descriptions are supplier statements. They are not proof of organic certification, pesticide-free production or any specific residue status. Such claims require current, lot-specific documents and test reports.
