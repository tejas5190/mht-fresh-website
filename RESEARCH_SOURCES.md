## Scope completed in this pass

- Apple: Gala, Fuji, Granny Smith, Red Delicious and Golden Delicious.
- Mandarin: Nadorcott, Tango, Clementine, Murcott, Nova, Satsuma and Orri (commodity-level evidence, not cultivar-specific).

## Interpretation and limitations

- UC Davis sources are postharvest research references. Some cultivar sheets are old; they are not substitutes for current origin-country protocols, buyer specifications, destination-market standards or a postharvest specialist's advice.
- Temperature, relative humidity, controlled-atmosphere and maturity figures are shown only in the context of the cited source. They must not be copied into a shipment instruction without checking cultivar/type, maturity, origin, transit duration, packaging and buyer requirements.
- California grape harvest SSC values are explicitly labelled as California-context references, not universal export limits.
- Nutrition pages do not show numeric nutrition facts because the reviewed technical references do not provide a cultivar-matched food-composition table.
- The origin lists in the catalogue are navigation examples, not verified seasonal availability, supplier relationships or Indian import permission.
- Export grades, size/count charts, carton weights, packaging formats, residue limits and actual lot measurements remain unpopulated until product-, supplier- and destination-specific evidence is obtained.
- Actual lot measurements must be kept separate from general reference data and should record sample size, date, instrument, method, units and responsible inspector.
