A little about us

Bringing the world of produce closer to India.

MHT Fresh is a fresh-produce trade initiative focused on connecting international suppliers with buyers and trade participants in India.

We believe good trade begins with clear information and dependable relationships. From product and origin to variety, packing and shipment requirements, we aim to make every conversation more informed.
