About MHT Fresh

Bringing the produce trade closer together.

MHT Fresh is building a connected platform for the fresh-produce trade — bringing international suppliers, Indian buyers and market opportunities into one conversation.

Our approach is grounded in transparent coordination, clear product information and relationships shaped around each trade partner's requirements.

We focus on practical communication around product, origin, quality expectations, packing, shipment and commercial terms.
