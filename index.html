OUR STORY

Bringing the world of produce closer to India.

MHT Fresh is a fresh-produce trade initiative connecting international suppliers with buyers and trade participants across India.

We work across product discovery, sourcing coordination and market connections. From origin and variety to packing, seasonality and shipment requirements, we believe informed conversations create stronger trade relationships.

Our focus is simple: build dependable partnerships and make fresh-produce trade more connected, transparent and accessible.

OUR PURPOSE

Our mission.

To create a dependable bridge between global fresh-produce suppliers and India's buyers by encouraging clear communication, informed sourcing and long-term trade relationships.

We aim to make quality produce more accessible while respecting the people, partnerships and processes behind every consignment.

OUR AMBITION

Our vision.

To become a trusted trade partner in the fresh-produce ecosystem, connecting growing regions with a wider network of Indian markets.

We believe sustainable growth comes from product understanding, responsible coordination, reliable supply relationships and a continued focus on quality.
